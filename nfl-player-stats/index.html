Did the 2025 rate carry into 2026?

Each player keeps his own 2025 mark and the band of his last 10 games of 2025. The 2026 columns count his regular-season games completed by 19 November 2026 at that same mark. Players with no 2026 game are left out. Nothing from 2026 chose a mark or a band.

Which players and games are here?

I kept quarterbacks, running backs, wide receivers and tight ends with at least 8 recorded games in the 2025 regular season and an exact median of at least 10 yards in their main stat. That stat is passing yards for QB, rushing yards for RB, and receiving yards for WR and TE. The median averages the two middle values when the game count is even.

A CSV row means a game played. Missing weeks add no game and no zero. A blank stat within a recorded row counts as zero. Only regular-season rows enter the calculations. January 2026 dates in these tables belong to the 2025 season. 2026 rows appear only in the 2026 sections and the carry table, and never change a 2025 mark, count or later check.

The last 5 and last 10 mean the final played games, with fewer than 10 when the log is shorter. Each count includes a value equal to the mark. Games column n and reaches k appear together. For the three card thresholds, take the exact 2025 median, round upward to a whole yard, then multiply by 0.75, 1 and 1.25. Round each result to the nearest 5 yards, with halfway values rounded upward. Small medians can give two cards the same threshold. The middle card is the rounded median mark used by splits and the later check. The chart line marks the exact median.

Home and away mean designated sides from games.csv, including neutral venues. Division means the opponents were division rivals in that recorded game. A player with more than one team keeps all his recorded games. Team lists name every team in his 2025 log. Empty split groups have no rate or range.

Wilson 95% ranges use z = 1.96 and endpoints rounded to whole percentages. They describe sampling doubt under a model of separate trials with a common rate. Shared opponents and changing conditions limit that model. Read the range calculation.

What held up later counts

The rounded 2025 median mark and the final last-10 rate band set each player’s question. Across his 2024 and 2025 regular-season games in date order, each past spot uses only the 10 played games ending there. The bands are under 40%, 40 to 59%, 60 to 69%, 70 to 79%, and 80% and over. A spot in the same band contributes up to 5 games played after it. The next spot is at least 5 played games later. Those later groups never overlap within a player’s check. Partial groups at the end stay in the count.

With fewer than 15 later games, the page says “too few past spots to tell” and hides the score and range. Otherwise it gives x of y and the Wilson range. The mark comes from the full 2025 season. This describes stored results; it is not a test of a number fixed before 2025.

The pooled player study uses a different question: a 2024 mark, 2025 spots and outcomes, and a six-game step between spots in the same band. Its group results are separate from these individual checks.

Questions

Why are some players absent?

They had fewer than 8 recorded 2025 games, a main-stat median below 10 yards, or a position outside QB, RB, WR and TE.

Do missed games count as zero?

No. Only recorded played games count. A player with 8 games has a last-10 count out of 8.

What does each index row compare?

Each row uses that player’s own rounded median mark. Players have different marks, listed beside the counts.
