THAT LAST ONE, AND WHY IT IS HERE

This engine reports where each of its numbers came from: whether a figure was
counted from data or is a stated prior, how many observations sit behind it,
and how stale the inputs are. Those labels are not decoration. A build that
strips them prints the same digits while destroying the only thing that makes
them worth reading, and it would still carry this project's name.
