What every trend shows

The number, how many games it rests on, and whether it lasted

Most stat sites show a percentage and stop. BookBreaker shows what that percentage is worth.

The game count

Every rate says how many games it comes from. 7 of 10 and 70 of 100 look the same as a percentage. They aren't the same.

A range, not one number

With only a few games, the true rate could be far from what they show. Each trend shows the range it could really sit in, so a small sample looks small.

Held up later

For each kind of trend, BookBreaker checks how often it kept holding in the games after it was spotted, game by game through the season, never with hindsight.

On your computer

No account, and your history never disappears

No sign-in

The app downloads one shared stats file, the same file for everyone. There's no profile to fill in and nothing to log in to.

Works offline

Everything runs on your Mac or PC, on the last update it has. Each update shows its date, so you always know how fresh the stats are.

Kept for good

Past seasons and past games stay on your computer. A stat you looked at last month is still there next month.

Questions

Before it comes out

Can I get BookBreaker now?

Not yet. It comes out on 18 December 2026, for Mac and Windows. This page will have the download and the price on it that day.

Which sports does it cover?

The NBA, NFL, MLB and NHL: player and team stats, game by game, with home and away, opponent and date filters.

What does “held up later” mean?

When a trend is spotted, BookBreaker keeps checking it in the games that come after. For each kind of trend, it shows how often trends like that kept holding, checked game by game through the season and never with hindsight.

Why does every number say how many games it rests on?

Because 7 of 10 and 70 of 100 are not the same thing. Over 10 games the true rate could be a long way from 70%, so BookBreaker shows the game count and the range the rate could really sit in, next to every number.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-in. The app downloads one shared stats file, keeps your history on your own computer, and works offline on the last update it has.

What happens after the first year?

The $39 includes 12 months of new games. After that, new games are an optional $19 a year. Without it, the app keeps every stat and tool it already has; only new games stop arriving.
