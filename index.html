How it works

Win without picking winners

Sportsbooks are built to profit whoever wins. Every so often two of them disagree enough that both sides can pay, if you get both bets on.

Betting both sides usually loses

Every book takes a cut. Bet $100 on each side of a -110 / -110 game and you lose either way.

Side one wins−$9.09
Side two wins−$9.09

Unless the prices disagree

Find +110 at one book and +105 at another. Bet $490 and $500, and if both bets are accepted at those prices, you win whichever side comes in.

Side one wins+$39.00
Side two wins+$35.00

Bookbreaker does the work

Finding those pairs by hand means watching prices all day. The app does it, then hands you the bet.

Questions

Before you download

Is the profit guaranteed?

No. It is locked only if both bets are accepted at the prices shown and both stand. A price can move before your second bet lands, a book can cap your stake or void a bet, and an exchange may have only a little on offer at the price you see. Live pairs are sized to the amount on offer when the prices were read, but that can change in seconds, so check before you bet. When we priced the Kalshi and Polymarket gaps our own scanner found against what was really on offer, nearly all of them disappeared, and the one left was worth about a cent. Real gaps are rare and small. That is why the app tells you how likely each price is to still be there.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no subscription, no account and no card. Download it and use it.

What is arbitrage betting?

Betting every side of a game at books whose prices disagree, so the winning bet pays more than the losing one costs. The full guide.

Where do the prices come from?

Switch on Live prices and the app reads Kalshi's and Polymarket US's public prices for NFL, MLB, NBA and NHL games, and shows a pair only in states where both are open. For the sportsbooks, add a free key from The Odds API (500 credits a month) and the app reads live odds from the major US books, saying what each refresh costs before it runs. Or type in the price you see and the app works out the rest.

Will betting like this get me limited?

Some books limit accounts that win. Bookbreaker rounds your stakes and shows which books in your state limit winners.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later). It is 10.2 MB and notarised by Apple.

Does it place bets for me?

No. It does the math and you place every bet yourself, at licensed books, in a state where betting is legal.
