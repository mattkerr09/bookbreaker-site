Questions

Before it comes out

Can I get BookBreaker now?

Not yet. BookBreaker is in development. There is no download and no launch date.

Do I need an account?

Coming BookBreaker will need no account and will keep your history on your computer.

What does held up later mean?

It means counting later games after similar past rates, with rules that keep future scores out of the past spot choice.

Coming BookBreaker will show how often similar past trends reached the number in later games, with a count and a Wilson range.
