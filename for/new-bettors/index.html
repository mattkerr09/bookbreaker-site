What actually decides whether betting is profitable?

Three facts decide whether betting is profitable, and none of them is picking winners.

The price already includes a fee

At the standard -110 both ways you need to win 52.38% just to break even. That is why a 50% bettor loses steadily and why the sportsbook does not need you to be wrong.

Your results prove almost nothing for a long time

A real 220-bet record showing 5.91% return reads as a success. Here is what it actually supports:

+5.91% flat-bet ROI, but the interval spans zero (-7.0% to +18.9%) — indistinguishable from break-even

The interval spans zero. Below 30 settled bets nothing is worth characterising at all — and slicing a record until something looks good makes it worse, not better.

The account is a resource

Books limit consistent winners, so an edge you cannot place is worth nothing. That makes stake shape, timing and market mix part of the arithmetic rather than a separate topic.
