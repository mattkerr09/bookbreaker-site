The 1 venue in West Virginia that never limit a winning account: Kalshi. On a list of 9, that is where a record worth having gets built.

With 8 online books taking bets, West Virginia is one of the deeper markets in the country. That depth is the edge: the gap between the best and second-best price on the same market is what arbitrage lives on, and it widens with every additional book willing to quote.

Of those, 8 will restrict an account that wins consistently and 1 will not. That is not a detail to discover later: it decides which account is worth building a real record at, and which are worth using for their prices while they last.

At 10% on sportsbook revenue, West Virginia is one of the lighter tax regimes. Operators have more room to price competitively here than in states taking three or four times as much, and that room is where a shopper's edge comes from.

West Virginia has had online betting since 2019-08, long enough for the promotional phase to be over and for risk desks to have a settled view of who they want as a customer. Accounts here get profiled by operators with years of data behind them.
