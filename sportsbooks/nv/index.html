The venues that cannot ban you

Prediction markets are regulated federally rather than by Nevada, which is why they appear in every state. They also have no bookmaker deciding who is allowed to keep betting, so they are structurally the best home for a consistent winner — and they can anchor a fair price where no sharp sportsbook operates.

Which to open first in Nevada

Open at least two. One account cannot be line shopped, and the gap between the best and second-best price on the same market is the entire edge in arbitrage and most of it in +EV betting.

If a book on that list never limits winners, it is the one to build a real record at. The rest are worth opening for their prices and their welcome offers, and worth treating as accounts with a lifespan.

Is online sports betting legal in Nevada?

Which sportsbooks in Nevada limit winning accounts?

2 of the 4 venues covering Nevada limit accounts that win consistently. 2 never do: Circa Sports, Kalshi.

How old do I have to be to bet?

21 or older in every state with legal sports betting, and you must be physically present in that state when the bet is placed.

How many sportsbooks do I need in Nevada?

At least two. A single account cannot be line shopped, and the whole arbitrage and +EV case rests on holding the best of several prices rather than whatever one book offers.
