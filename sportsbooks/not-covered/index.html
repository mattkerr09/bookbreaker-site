States this table does not cover

10 states have legal betting that the operator table behind this site does not describe. They share a page because the honest answer is the same in each: the market exists and we have not catalogued it.

  • Delaware — lottery-run — not in the AS_OF source
  • Mississippi — retail only at time of source
  • Montana — lottery-run kiosk/app — not in the AS_OF source
  • North Dakota — tribal retail only at time of source
  • New Hampshire — lottery-run, single operator — not in the AS_OF source
  • New Mexico — tribal retail only at time of source
  • Oregon — lottery-run, single operator — not in the AS_OF source
  • Rhode Island — lottery-run — not in the AS_OF source
  • South Dakota — retail only at time of source
  • Wisconsin — tribal retail only at time of source

Most run a lottery-operated book, which is a different shape from the multi-operator markets elsewhere — often one app, sometimes kiosk-only. Naming the gap matters more than it might seem: an empty list and an uncatalogued market look identical to a reader, and only one of them is a bug.
