Pennsylvania sports betting FAQ

Is online sports betting legal in Pennsylvania?

Pennsylvania had 11 licensed online sportsbooks operating as of 2026-08-17, alongside 1 federally regulated prediction market.

Which sportsbooks in Pennsylvania limit winning accounts?

11 of the 12 venues covering Pennsylvania limit accounts that win consistently. Kalshi never do.

How many sportsbooks should I open in Pennsylvania?

At least two of the 11 available. One account cannot be line shopped, and the gap between the best and second-best price is the entire edge in arbitrage.
