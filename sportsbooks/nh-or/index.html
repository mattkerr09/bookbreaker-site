Sportsbooks you can use in New Hampshire and Oregon

2 venues reachable from New Hampshire and Oregon, of which 1 are retail sportsbooks and 1 can anchor a fair price.

These 2 states are on one page because they license exactly the same set of venues — separate pages would have said the same thing twice.

New Hampshire and Oregon is a single-operator market — an exclusive lottery contract with DraftKings. DraftKings is the only retail sportsbook here, which makes it the hardest kind of market to arb: an arbitrage needs two books and there is one. What remains is the exchange, and pricing DraftKings against it.
