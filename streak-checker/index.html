Did the streak hold? A free NFL player streak checker

Choose a player and a yardage number. The checker counts how often he reached it in his last 5 or 10 games, with a range, then looks back at his own past runs at the same rate and counts what came in the next 5 games.

It holds 667 NFL quarterbacks, running backs, wide receivers and tight ends, with their regular-season games from 2024 to 2026. The range is the Wilson 95% range: how far the true rate could plausibly be from this count, given so few games.

How the later check works

The rate in your window falls in one of five bands: under 40%, 40 to 59%, 60 to 69%, 70 to 79%, and 80% and over. The checker walks through the player's games in date order. At each game it reads only the window ending there. When that rate falls in your band, it counts the next 5 games he played, then skips ahead past them, so no later game counts twice. Under 15 later games, it says so instead of showing a rate.

Across every NFL player we checked, high recent rates came back toward the middle in the games after. Read the pooled player study and the 2025 to 2026 carry table.

Questions

Where do the numbers come from?

From nflverse weekly player statistics for the 2024, 2025 and 2026 regular seasons, joined to the nflverse schedules file for dates and opponents. The 2026 file was read 2026-11-24 and is read again each Tuesday during the season.

Which number does each player use?

Passing yards for quarterbacks, rushing yards for running backs, and receiving yards for wide receivers and tight ends, by the position on his latest recorded game. The box starts at his median over his latest 17 games, rounded to the nearest 5 yards. Type any number you like.

What counts as a game?

A recorded regular-season game. Missed weeks add no game and no zero. A game equal to the number counts as reaching it. Postseason games are left out.

Is anything I type sent anywhere?

No. The page loads one data file and does the counting in your browser. There is no form to submit and nothing is stored.
