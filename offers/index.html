Every sign-up bonus, and what it is really worth

Ranked by the cash you keep, not the headline amount. A bonus bet is worth what you can lock in by hedging it at a second book, if both bets are accepted and stand. Ranked that way, the order changes.

Priced by the engine at build time, on two stated priors: bonus bets placed at decimal 4.0, and a 2.0% combined hold across the best two books. The app lets you change both.
