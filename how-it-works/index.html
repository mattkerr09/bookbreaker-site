How Bookbreaker works

One app with a handful of jobs. Each one answers a question bettors actually ask, with the math already done.

Ask

Ask in plain English

Type a question the way you would say it: who is most likely to pass for 300 yards this week, or what the best ten parlays are if you have ten $1 bets and want $200. Every number in the answer comes from the engine, not from the AI's memory: passing-yard chances from a projection whose stated chances were checked against what really happened in seasons it was not tuned on, the plain arithmetic of a split bet, fair odds, and arbs and +EV bets from your own odds feed. With no AI key it still answers questions like these. Add your own Anthropic key, or run a model on your Mac with Ollama, and it understands any question. It never places a bet.

Arbitrage

See a pair the way you will bet it

The Arbitrage page shows both books side by side: the price at each, what to bet at each, and what you make if each side wins. The profit is locked only if both bets are accepted and stand. Switch on Live prices and it fills with real Kalshi and Polymarket US pairs, sized to what is on offer; with it off, it shows one demonstration market.

Arb calculator

Know exactly what to bet at each book

Enter two prices and how much you want to put in. You get the stake for each book, rounded to amounts a book will take, and the profit locked if both bets are accepted and stand.

+110 and +105 on $1,000: bet $490 and $500, and keep at least $35.00 if both stand.

Will it fill?

Check a price is still there

The price on your screen is already a few seconds old. Bookbreaker adds the feed's own delay and tells you the chance the price is still open when your bet lands, so you stop chasing prices that are gone.

84%chance a price that looks 10 seconds old is still there, once the feed's 5.4 second delay is counted
Bet size

Bet the right amount

Tell it your bankroll and your edge. It gives a bet size that grows your bankroll without betting it all, and says what capped it.

Full Kelly says 8.33% of the bankroll. The app stakes 2.08%, because a small error in your edge costs far more when you bet big.

Parlay

See what a parlay really pays

Type the legs and it shows what the parlay pays against what it should. A 4-leg parlay at −110 pays 12.28 to 1 when a fair price would be 15.00 to 1.

Promos

Turn sign-up bonuses into cash

Bonus bets do not pay back their stake, so most people get half their value. Bookbreaker ranks welcome offers by what you keep after hedging, and walks you through each bet. See this week's offers.

My bets

Know if you are actually winning

Import your sportsbook's export or paste a betslip. It shows your return and whether your record proves anything yet. A 220-bet record up 5.91% can still be luck, and the app says so.

220bets in the example record, and still too few to tell skill from luck

Where the prices come from

Switch on Live prices and the app reads Kalshi's and Polymarket US's public prices. For a sportsbook, you type the price you see and the app does the rest. It knows 33 sportsbooks, exchanges and prediction markets, and which of them take bets in each state: 15 in New Jersey, 3 in Florida. Check your state.

What it never does

It never asks for a sportsbook login and never places a bet for you. Your record comes from the export your book already gives you. It does nothing about identity, location or device: keeping an account healthy is done with the shape of the bet, nothing else. How it keeps accounts open.

How old the price already is

A quote that looks 10 seconds old is really 15.4, because the feed took 5.4 seconds to reach you and those seconds were invisible. The hatched region is the part nobody showed you:

Fill probability follows the real age, not the shown one: 89% becomes 84%, and a 4.0% screen edge is worth 3.37% rather than 3.58%.

Middles, counted rather than assumed

Results are not smoothly distributed. Football margins pile up on 3 and 7 because of how the sport scores, so a normal curve gets the shape wrong in exactly the place the whole bet lives. Same prices, one point apart:
