What are the true odds behind a sportsbook's price?

A book offering -145 / +125 is asserting probabilities that sum to more than one. Removing that margin gives the favourite:

additive57.37%
multiplicative57.11%
power57.50%
shin57.37%

Consensus 57.34%, and the four methods disagree by 0.39 points. Every other no-vig calculator returns one number.

Where the number comes from
