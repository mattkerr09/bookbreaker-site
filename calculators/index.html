Betting calculators, worked rather than blank

Every calculator page on the internet shows you a form and a formula. These show the arithmetic already done on real prices, and the range the answer sits in — because the range is the part that decides whether a bet is worth taking.

  • No-vig odds calculatorWhat are the true odds behind a sportsbook's price?
  • Expected value calculatorIs this bet +EV?
  • Arbitrage calculatorHow much do I stake on each side?
  • Kelly criterion calculatorHow much should I bet?
  • Hold and vig calculatorHow much is this book charging me?
  • Bonus bet conversion calculatorWhat is my free bet actually worth?
  • Middle calculatorWhat does this middle need to hit to be worth it?
  • Odds converterAmerican to decimal to implied probability
  • Break-even calculatorWhat win rate does this price need?
  • CLV calculatorDid I beat the closing line?

All of them are the engine that prices bets, not a separate implementation. A calculator that disagrees with the product it advertises is worse than no calculator.
