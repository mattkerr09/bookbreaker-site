What does this middle need to hit to be worth it?

Identical prices, one point apart:

WindowHitsBreak-evenExpected value
2.5 / 3.515.79%4.76%+10.53%
4.5 / 5.54.21%4.76%-0.53%

Break-even is arithmetic on the two prices and depends on no distribution at all, which makes it the honest anchor. The hit rate comes from counting 285 recorded games — a synthetic, shape stated sample shown as an illustration of the mechanism, not a claim about any league.

Where the number comes from

Everything above was computed by the same engine that prices bets, at the moment this page was built — not typed into a template. The build fails if a figure appears here and not in the engine's own output.
