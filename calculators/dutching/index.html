Dutching calculator

How do I split a stake across several outcomes?

Dutch your own outcomes

Type the total you want to stake and the price of every outcome you are covering. The stakes below make each one pay the same.

300 spread across 3 outcomes so that every result pays the same:

PriceStakeReturns
+120131.83290.03
+24085.30290.03
+25082.87290.03

Every branch returns 290.03 on 300 staked: -9.97, or -3.32%. That is negative here, and it is supposed to be: the prices imply 103.44% of probability between them, and everything above a hundred is the book's margin. Dutching is the same solver as an arbitrage without the locked floor.

So it earns nothing on its own. It is a way of expressing a view you already hold (that the winner is somewhere inside this set) at a known cost. It is not a way of manufacturing an edge. A dutch across outcomes you have no opinion about is a slow donation at a rate you can compute in advance.

Where the number comes from

The worked example was computed by the same engine that prices bets, at the moment this page was built. None of it was typed in. The build fails if a figure appears here and not in the engine's own output. The calculator runs that engine, release 0.1.8, in your browser, and the build checks that it returns this example’s answers for this example’s inputs.
