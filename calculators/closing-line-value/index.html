Did I beat the closing line?

A bet taken at +110 against a market that closed at -105:

Your price+110
Closing price-105
CLV against the raw close+7.56%

That figure compares two vigged prices and so understates the real edge by the closing margin — usable for ranking bets against each other, not for claiming an edge size. Devigging the closing market first is what the engine does, and it is why closing line value converges far faster than profit.

Where the number comes from

Everything above was computed by the same engine that prices bets, at the moment this page was built — not typed into a template. The build fails if a figure appears here and not in the engine's own output.
