How much do I stake on each side?

+110 at one book and +105 at another is a 3.73% arbitrage on 1,000 staked:

Exact stakes493.98 / 506.02
Guaranteed37.35
Round stakes490 / 500
Guaranteed35.00
Cost of looking human2.35

A stake to the cent is the loudest fingerprint a risk desk reads. This solves for round stakes directly, because rounding a lock afterwards breaks it.
