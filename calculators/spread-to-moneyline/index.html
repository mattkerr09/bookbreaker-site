NFL spread to moneyline converter

By Matt Kerr · Updated 6 November 2026

About -148 with the margin removed, from the average of the closing moneylines on every NFL game since 2015 that closed with a 3-point spread. Each spread has its own moneyline, and the steps between them jump at 3 and 7, where football margins pile up. This page gives the fair moneyline for every spread with enough games behind it, and how often those favourites actually won.

Convert your own spread

Enter an NFL point spread, like -3 or 6.5.

A spread and a moneyline price the same game two ways, so each spread has a moneyline it matches. The cleanest way to find it is to ask the market. Across 3023 NFL games from 2015 to 2025 that closed with both a spread and moneylines, a 3-point favourite’s closing moneylines, with the margin removed, gave it a 59.74% chance on average: a fair moneyline of -148, and +148 for the underdog. At 7 points it was 74.14%, -287. From nflverse’s public games file: source, read 2026-10-22.

The table checks that against what happened. A 3-point favourite won outright 259 of 432, 60.0%, and the range that rate could sit in, given the sample, is 55.3% to 64.5%. Where the outright rate is far from the market’s chance, look at the range first: most spreads closed in only a few dozen to a few hundred games.

These are fair prices. A book’s posted moneyline adds its margin on top, so the favourite’s price is a little longer and the underdog’s a little shorter than the table. The steps are not even, either: the chance jumps at 3 and 7, where football margins pile up. The key numbers show how often each margin lands.
