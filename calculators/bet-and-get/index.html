Bet and get calculator

By Matt Kerr · Updated 6 November 2026

Most of the headline, if you convert the bonus bets well. The bonus arrives whatever the qualifying bet does, so the qualifying bet is a small fee and nearly all the value is in how much of each bonus bet you turn into cash. When the bonus is paid only if the qualifying bet wins, its price decides how often you collect. This page works out both.

Value your own bet-and-get

Enter the qualifying stake, its price and the other side's price at the same book, the bonus and the share of it you expect to keep in cash.

Take the common form: bet $5, get $200 in bonus bets, paid whatever the qualifying bet does. Placed at -110 on a market priced -110 and -110, the qualifier loses $0.23 on average, which is the margin on $5. That is the whole cost.

The value is in the conversion. Bet each bonus bet at +200 and cover the other side at -230, and the engine keeps 60.6% of the bonus in cash, if every bet is accepted and stands: the $200 becomes $121.20. Take off the qualifier and the offer is worth $120.97, and the qualifier’s cost is 0.19% of it. A rate you get at better prices moves the answer more than anything else on this page.

When the bonus is paid only if the qualifier wins

Some books pay the bonus only if the qualifying bet wins. Now the qualifier’s chance decides how often you collect, so a short price is worth more: the opposite of a no-sweat bet. Below, bet $10 to get $150 if it wins, at the same 60.6% conversion:

Qualifying priceOther sideChance it winsBonus received, on averageWorth, on average
-500+38081.11%$121.67$73.47
-110-11050.00%$75.00$45.00
+150-17038.56%$57.84$34.69

Each chance is that market’s own prices with the margin removed, the average of four methods, which is an estimate. This version cannot be hedged: a bet against the qualifier cannot pay out bonus bets, so in the branch where it loses you get nothing.

Two things the headline leaves out. Bonus bets often arrive in several pieces that each expire, so the conversion has to happen in time. And a bonus bet returns only its winnings, never its own stake, which is why it converts for less than its face.
