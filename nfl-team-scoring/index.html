How these pages count games

I built these tables from completed regular-season games, ordered by date and game ID. Each played game appears once for each team. Postseason games are left out. The season label is the season year, so January dates can belong to the prior year’s season.

At or above includes a score equal to the threshold. Each team’s three thresholds use its own season median rounded up, then 7 points below and above it. Last 5 and last 10 use the final played games. Splits use that median threshold and count each filtered group separately. Home and away are the file’s designated sides, including at neutral venues. Overtime points stay in the final score.

The Wilson 95% range uses z = 1.96 and rounds endpoints to whole percentages. It estimates uncertainty about a common underlying rate under a model of separate trials. Related games and changing conditions limit that model. Read the range calculation and its limits.

The later-games check

Each team’s median threshold and final last-10 rate band set the question. The past search joins only that team’s 2024 and 2025 regular seasons. It starts after a full 10 played games. A spot uses only the 10 games ending there. The next 5 played games supply the later count, with partial groups kept at the end. After selecting a spot, the next spot starts no earlier than the end of those 5 games. No later game counts twice within that team’s check.

A final-game spot adds no later games. With fewer than 15 later games, the page says “too few past spots to tell” and withholds the score and range. Counts are pooled within one team, rather than across the league. This is a description of these stored seasons. The threshold itself comes from the full 2025 season, so this is not a test of a threshold chosen before that season. Read PLAN-FIXES section 1 as applied in the worked study.

The two division links on each page go to its two most recently faced distinct division opponents in the displayed log.

The schedules file was read 2026-10-22. Its 2026 season rows stop at that read date, so the 2026 season is not used anywhere in these calculations.

Questions

Are these player statistics?

These are team scores. There are no player logs in this dataset batch.

Can I compare the median thresholds across teams?

The thresholds differ by team. Use the index’s 24-point column for a shared scoring mark.

Where are the source rows?

Each team page has every included 2025 game. The Sources list names the schedules file and the dated method notes.
