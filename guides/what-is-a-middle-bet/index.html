What is a middle bet

How can two bets both win?

Bet over 2.5 at one book and under 3.5 at another. If the result lands between them, both bets win. Outside, one wins and one loses, so the position costs a little — and the middle is the payoff.

Everything hinges on how often the result lands in the window, and this is where nearly every tool is casually wrong. Results are not smoothly distributed: football margins pile up on 3 and 7 because of how the sport scores. Same prices, one point apart:

WindowHitsBreak-evenExpected value
2.5 / 3.515.79%4.76%+10.53%
4.5 / 5.54.21%4.76%-0.53%

A normal curve prices those identically. The break-even column is the honest anchor — arithmetic on the two prices, depending on no distribution at all.
