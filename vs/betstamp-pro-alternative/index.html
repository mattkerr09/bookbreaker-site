The gap

No published measure of how often a surfaced line is still there.

That absence is not incidental. A screen sorted by raw expected value is sorted partly by how stale its own data is, because the biggest numbers cluster on the books that moved most recently — which are the books most likely to have moved again.

What Betstamp PRO costs you before you win anything

At ~$347/mo (demo-gated), the cheapest monthly plan is $347.00 — $4,164.00 a year. That is not a price you pay out of profit. It is a hurdle you clear before there is any profit, and it is worth seeing as the number of winning bets it takes to reach zero.

Who Betstamp PRO is actually for

At $347 a month this is priced for someone betting full time. The subscription only makes sense if you are already turning over $17,350 a month at a real edge, and if you are not there yet, the fee is the largest negative-EV bet in your month.
