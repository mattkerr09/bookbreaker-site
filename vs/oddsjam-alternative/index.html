What OddsJam costs you before you win anything

At Gold $199.99/mo; Global $399.99/mo, the cheapest monthly plan is $199.99 — $2,399.88 a year. That is not a price you pay out of profit. It is a hurdle you clear before there is any profit, and it is worth seeing as the number of winning bets it takes to reach zero.

Who OddsJam is actually for

$200 a month is a serious hobbyist price, and clearing it needs 100 winning bets a month at a $100 stake and a 2% edge. That is reachable, which is exactly why it is worth checking against your own record rather than assuming.

The question is not whether the tool is good. It is whether your volume clears its price, and that is arithmetic you can do before you subscribe.
