OddsJam vs Unabated

By Matt Kerr · Updated 4 November 2026

OddsJam and Unabated solve different problems. OddsJam offers pre-match bet recommendations for 109+ sportsbooks and live bets with auto refresh, and Unabated offers sharp tools built by professional bettors around the Unabated Line. Choose by the markets you bet and by what a plan costs you before any profit: OddsJam lists Sharp Money $6.60/day; the plan with arbitrage, +EV, middles, low hold and the promo converter $20/day ($8.30/day the first month), both billed monthly (read 2026-11-04, source), and Unabated lists Props+ $99/mo; Premium $199/mo; Concierge $799/mo; 14-day refund on monthly plans (read 2026-11-04, source).

Which one fits

OddsJam is a scanner: it surfaces arbitrage, +EV, middles and low holds across many books and refreshes live bets on its own, so it suits someone who wants a long list of plays to work through. Unabated is built around one fair line of its own making, with props tools on its cheapest plan, so it suits someone who wants a single sharp price to bet against rather than a feed of alerts. If you mostly bet player props, the Unabated props plan is the narrower and cheaper fit; if you want every opportunity type in one place, OddsJam covers more of them. (From each tool's own pages: OddsJam read 2026-11-04, source; Unabated read 2026-11-04, source.)

What each costs before any profit

OddsJam lists Sharp Money $6.60/day; the plan with arbitrage, +EV, middles, low hold and the promo converter $20/day ($8.30/day the first month), both billed monthly (read 2026-11-04, source). It publishes no monthly plan price, so its yearly cost before any profit cannot be worked out from what it publishes.

Unabated's cheapest monthly plan costs $1,188 a year. At a $100 stake and a two percent edge that is 50 bets a month, every month, just to break even on average (read 2026-11-04, source).

What neither says on its pricing page

OddsJam: publishes no fill rate and no latency figure (read 2026-11-04, source). Unabated: one fair line rather than the spread the devig methods actually allow (read 2026-11-04, source).
