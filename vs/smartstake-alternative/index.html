What Bookbreaker does instead

It is free, so there is no hurdle to clear before the first winning bet, and it reports what it does not know. On a -145 / +125 moneyline the four standard devig methods land between 57.11% and 57.50% — a spread of 0.39 points on a market where a 2% edge is a good day. Every tool in this category picks one method and prints the result to two decimals as though it were measured.
